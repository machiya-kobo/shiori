Search

Type sh, a space and your words in the address bar: your pages come up as you type, and Enter opens Shiori Search. To make Shiori a search engine of Firefox's own, open your hosted Shiori Search page and add it from the address bar. Shiori Search's gear holds the rest: theme, text size, tabs and how many results.
